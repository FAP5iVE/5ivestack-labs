## Part A: Owner notes and compliance map (not for publication)

### A1. Does the Malawi Act apply to you, and since when?

- The Act applies to processing of personal data in Malawi by a controller or processor who is domiciled, ordinarily resident or operating in Malawi (section 3(1)(a)). It also reaches controllers outside Malawi who offer goods or services to people in Malawi (section 3(1)(b)).
- Section 53 gave controllers that are **not** "of significant importance" a 24-month exemption from the Act, counted from the date it came into operation. Secondary sources report that date as **3 June 2024**, which would make the exemption end around **3 June 2026**. That is already past, so treat the Act as fully applicable. Confirm the commencement date in the Gazette notice.
- Authority: the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) is designated as the Data Protection Authority (section 4).

### A2. What the Act requires, and where this draft meets it

| Requirement | Section | Where it is met |

### A3. Owner actions

1. **Record of processing (section 29).** Keep a short written record with: controller name and contact, purpose, categories of people and data, categories of recipients, planned time limits for erasure, and a general description of security measures.
2. **Processor terms (section 32).** For each provider (hosting, email delivery, email inbox), accept the provider's data processing agreement in its account settings and keep a copy.
3. **Breach procedure (sections 36, 37).** Who decides, who contacts MACRA within 72 hours, and a template message to affected people.
4. **Retention decision.** Choose real retention periods for Part B section 7.
5. **Registration (section 41).** Required only for controllers "of significant importance", meaning more than 10,000 data subjects resident in Malawi, or data of significance to the economy, society or security of Malawi. A company website collecting enquiries is very unlikely to qualify. Re-check if volumes grow.
6. **Data protection officer (section 33)** and **impact assessments (section 30).** These are required for large-scale systematic monitoring and similar cases. This website does not trigger them. **CCTV installation work for clients is a separate question**: monitoring of publicly accessible areas at scale is a listed trigger for the person operating the cameras. Take advice on whether 5iveStack Labs acts as a processor under client contracts (section 32), and keep that separate from this website notice.
7. **MACRA contact details.** I could not verify a current public address or web page for complaints from the Act itself. Get them from MACRA directly before publishing.
8. **Legal entity details.** Registered name, registration number and address for Part B section 1.
9. **If you later add analytics, advertising cookies or a newsletter,** this notice and the consent approach must change first.

### A4. Other laws visitors may bring with them

- **EU and EEA visitors:** the GDPR can apply when a business offers services to people in the EU (Article 3(2)). If you actively target the EU, an EU representative may be needed (Article 27). If you do not, say nothing more than Part B already does.
- **UK visitors:** UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, supervised by the Information Commissioner's Office.
- **Other countries** (for example South Africa's POPIA, Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, Nigeria's NDPA 2023): similar principles apply. The notice below is written to the common worldwide standard (identity, purposes, lawful basis, recipients, transfers, retention, rights, complaint route), so it should serve them too. Counsel should confirm before you actively market in those countries.

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|

We do not ask for sensitive personal data (as defined in section 2 of the Act: biometric data, race or ethnic origin, religious or other belief, health status, political opinion). Please do not include any in your messages.

Our services are for businesses and organisations. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Where a child's data is involved and consent is the basis, the Act requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian (section 17).

## 4. Consent

Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time by emailing us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew (Act, section 14(3) and (4); GDPR Article 7(3)). We will ask for your consent separately for each purpose, and we do not make the service depend on consent we do not need.

## 5. Who receives your data

We share personal data only with providers who process it for us under written terms, and only as needed:

- **Hosting:** Vercel Inc., which hosts this website.
- **Email delivery:** [email delivery provider, to be named when selected], which sends your form submission to our inbox.
- **Email inbox:** Google (Gmail), where we receive and answer enquiries.
- **WhatsApp:** if you choose to message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp (Meta) processes that conversation under its own terms and privacy policy. Please read them before you use it.

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose it where a law or a court order requires.

## 6. Transfers outside Malawi

Yes. Our hosting and email providers may process personal data outside Malawi, including in the United States. We transfer personal data only where the recipient is subject to a law, binding corporate rules, contractual clauses or another mechanism that gives an adequate level of protection, or where another condition in section 39(4) of the Act applies (Act, sections 38 and 39). For people in the UK and EEA we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (GDPR Article 46). We keep a record of the basis for each transfer (section 38(2)).

## 7. How long we keep it

- Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: up to [24 months] after our last contact, then deleted or made anonymous.
- If we enter into a contract with you: for the life of the contract and then for [the period required by law or by our legal obligations].
- Server logs: [30 days, to be confirmed with the hosting provider].

We do not keep personal data longer than necessary for its purpose (Act, section 12).

## 8. How we protect it

We use HTTPS encryption in transit, limit access to people who need it, and collect only what we need. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Authority within 72 hours and, where there is a high risk to you, tell you within 72 hours (Act, sections 36 and 37).

## 9. Your rights

Under Part IV of the Act (sections 19 to 25), and under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR where it applies, you have the right to:

- **Access** your personal data and receive a copy, normally within 30 days and free of charge where practicable (section 19).
- **Receive it in a portable format** and have it sent to another controller (section 20).
- **Correct** errors or complete incomplete data. We act within 14 days (section 21).
- **Have it erased** in the circumstances the Act lists (section 22).
- **Restrict** our processing (section 23).
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing (section 24).
- **Not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing** that has a legal or similarly significant effect (section 25). We make no such decisions.

To use any right, email fivestacklabs@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. Some rights can be limited in the cases listed in section 26 of the Act.

## 10. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, please contact us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain, in writing, to the Data Protection Authority, which is the Malawi Communications Regulatory Authority (MACRA) [contact details to be confirmed with MACRA]. A complaint should be lodged within 90 days of the action or inaction you complain about (Act, section 44). You may also bring a civil claim for loss or harm caused by a breach of the Act (section 47). If you live in the UK or EEA, you may complain to your local data protection supervisory authority (GDPR Article 77).

## 11. Links to other services

Links to WhatsApp, email and other services are provided for convenience. Their own privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

## 12. Changes to this notice

We will publish changes on this page and update the date above. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we will tell you before it takes effect where we can.
